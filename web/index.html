                          About Puffin

                          Puffin

                          A personal music and video library for the devices you use every day.

                          Bring your media together, organize it with playlists, update its details, and keep track of what you listen to. Puffin is designed to feel at home on Mac and iPhone.

                          • Your library stays yours.Media and playlists are stored in this device’s browser storage.
                          • Play offline.Once media is saved to your library, you can play it without an internet connection.
                          • Choose how to download.Download direct media links on this device, or configure your Raspberry Pi for YouTube downloads.

                          Puffin is a personal project. Your library stays on each device and does not automatically sync between devices.
