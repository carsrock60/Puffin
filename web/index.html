                                  Privacy

                                  Puffin keeps your library in this browser. This page lists every way data leaves the device.

                                  Stays on this device

                                  • Media, artwork, subtitles, and playlistsSaved in this browser’s storage (IndexedDB). They are never uploaded to Puffin or synced to another device.
                                  • Listening history and statisticsRecently played, watch progress, play counts, and library totals stay in this browser. Discover sends only a compact seed profile, described below.
                                  • Settings and API keysYour AcoustID key is saved in this browser only. Clearing site data erases it along with your library.

                                  Downloads on this device

                                  In the Puffin desktop app, YouTube search and media downloads are performed by yt-dlp and FFmpeg running on your computer. Puffin sends the search phrase or video URL, the media type, the requested resolution, the subtitle language, and the audio formats the app can play only to YouTube. Media is written to a private temporary folder, copied into your Puffin library, and removed afterward. Everything stays on this computer.

                                  Sent to other services

                                  • YouTubeReceives the search or video request directly from the Puffin desktop app. The website itself does not download media.
                                  • Puffin’s discovery serviceReceives only a compact profile: up to 8 artist names, up to 2 track titles per artist, coarse play and library counts, and names to avoid. It never receives your media files, playlists, or raw listening history.
                                  • Last.fmReceives an artist name or a seed track title from Puffin’s discovery service to find similar music. The project key stays on the server.
                                  • Fanart.tvReceives a MusicBrainz artist ID from Puffin’s discovery service to find an artist photo. The project key stays on the server.
                                  • AcoustIDReceives an audio fingerprint, the track duration, and your application key when identifying songs. The audio file itself is never uploaded.
                                  • MusicBrainz and Cover Art ArchiveReceive artist and title searches and release artwork requests during Discover, Smart Fill, and metadata editing.
                                  • LRCLIBReceives artist and title to look up lyrics.
                                  • iTunes Search APIReceives artist and album names to find artwork for Discover albums.
                                  • Puffin’s Smart Fill service (Groq)Receives the raw track title and candidate lists to rank matches. The Groq key stays on that server and is never stored in this browser.
                                  • VercelServes the Puffin app and its Smart Fill and Discover endpoints; your library is not part of those requests.
                                  • GitHubReceives a version check from the desktop app when it looks for updates. No library data is sent.

                                  What never leaves

                                  Your media files, playlists, full library, and raw listening history are never uploaded. Discover sends only the compact seed profile described above, and each service receives only the small text, query, or identifier described. Every service handles it under its own privacy policy.

                                  About Puffin

                                  Puffin

                                  A personal music and video library for the devices you use every day.

                                  Bring your media together, organize it with playlists, update its details, and keep track of what you listen to. Puffin is designed to feel at home on Mac and iPhone.

                                  • Your library stays yours.Media and playlists are stored in this device’s browser storage.
                                  • Play offline.Once media is saved to your library, you can play it without an internet connection.
                                  • On-device downloads.The desktop app searches YouTube and downloads media locally with its own downloader. No extra hardware or private server is needed.

                                  Puffin is a personal project. Your library stays on each device and does not automatically sync between devices.
